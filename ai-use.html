AI use

We used an AI coding assistant (Claude Code, by Anthropic) for parts of this work. This page says exactly what it did and how we check it.

Our rules for AI output: AI never counts as a source. It did not invent sources, statistics, quotes or interviews. Anything it suggested is marked [VERIFY: open the source] or [TODO: ...] until a person checks the original.

On small screens, scroll the table sideways.

How to read the labels

  • Evidence we saw it in a listed source
  • Analysis our interpretation of evidence
  • Assumption our belief, not yet tested
  • Simulated made up for planning, not real people
  • Planned a check we have not done yet
  • [VERIFY] must be checked against the source
  • [TODO] still to be filled in
