Persona 1: “Jordan,” CS student looking for side income

Situation
Simulated Third-year computer science student, part-time campus job, comfortable with JavaScript and Python.
Goal
Simulated Earn a few hundred dollars a month from something they built, and have a project to show employers.
Frustrations
Simulated Doesn't know which idea to pick, how to find a first customer or how to take payments. Finds success stories intimidating.
Constraints
Simulated Little money to spend on tools or memberships. Time comes in bursts around exams.
What would help
Simulated A step-by-step “first $100” path, free tools and realistic examples from people like them.
SIMULATED / ASSUMPTION: not a real interview

Persona 2: “Sam,” new grad developer with a day job

Situation
Simulated Graduated within the last two years, works full time as a junior developer.
Goal
Simulated Build a small product on nights and weekends that could someday replace part of their salary.
Frustrations
Simulated Limited evening energy, unsure about marketing and pricing, worried about their employer's rules on side projects.
Constraints
Simulated Can afford some paid tools, but has very little time.
What would help
Simulated Time-boxed plans, pricing guidance and examples of products one person can maintain.
